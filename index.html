Mini Dictionary

A simple student-level mini project that stores a limited number of words from a specific domain along with their meanings. It enables users to search for a specific word and view the complete contents of the dictionary.

It allows only administrators, after a security check, to update, delete or add contents to the dictionary.

Procurement Status System

A project focusing on providing complete and transparent information about the processes involved in crop procurement that are not visible to farmers.

It was planned to improve transparency and reduce waiting time in the procurement queue. The system considers farmers who may have difficulty using digital applications and aims to reduce their difficulties by providing complete visibility and tracking of the payment process.

The project also considers location-based planning to help farmers plan their journey from the source to the destination and minimize waiting time. It includes multilingual support and voice-based notifications.
